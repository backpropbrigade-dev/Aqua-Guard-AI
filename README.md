# 🌊 AquaGuard AI

### AI-Powered Water Pollution Monitoring & Response Platform

AquaGuard AI is a technology-driven environmental platform focused on identifying, monitoring, and responding to pollution in oceans, rivers, lakes, and coastal regions. By integrating artificial intelligence, UAV-assisted surveillance, community participation, and environmental analytics, the platform enables faster detection, smarter decision-making, and more effective cleanup operations.

## ❓ Problem Statement

Water bodies across the world are increasingly affected by plastic waste, dumping activities, and environmental neglect. Detecting pollution early, coordinating response teams, and maintaining visibility over cleanup activities remain significant challenges for environmental organizations and government agencies.

Existing approaches often depend on disconnected systems, manual reporting, and delayed intervention processes, reducing the overall effectiveness of environmental protection efforts and limiting public participation.

---

## 💡 Solution

AquaGuard AI establishes a connected environmental response network that bridges the gap between pollution detection and corrective action. Through AI-assisted analysis, location intelligence, UAV support, mission coordination tools, and stakeholder collaboration, the platform enables environmental incidents to be identified, prioritized, and resolved more efficiently.

### 👥 Citizen Reporting

Allow citizens to report pollution incidents through image uploads, location tagging, and AI-assisted environmental analysis.

### 🚁 UAV Monitoring

Support surveillance operations and investigate high-priority pollution cases using UAV-assisted monitoring and automated dispatch workflows.

### 🏢 Authority Verification

Enable authorities to review, validate, prioritize, and manage pollution reports before initiating cleanup actions.

### 🌱 Cleanup Mission Management

Coordinate cleanup campaigns through NGO partners and field teams while tracking mission progress and environmental impact.

## ✨ Key Features

### 🤖 AI-Powered Pollution Detection

Analyze uploaded images and reports to identify plastic waste, estimate severity levels, and support environmental monitoring efforts.

Manage cleanup campaigns, field missions, volunteer coordination, and mission verification workflows.

Track pollution trends, mission performance, regional impact metrics, and operational effectiveness through data-driven dashboards.
